🔒 Data Privacy & How Your Information Is Handled

We respect your privacy and designed this application to keep your personal data strictly under your control:

  • Local Storage in Your Google Drive: All your personal data—including dietary preferences, allergies, custom recipes, meal history, and approved meal plans—is stored exclusively in your private Google Drive in the Automated_Meal_Planner_DB.json file. No external database or unauthorized party can access your information.
  • AI Generation & Processing: When generating meal plans or recipes, only the necessary planning criteria (dietary restrictions, diner count, preferred/avoided cuisines, and requested meal count) are sent securely to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). No identity credentials, emails, or unrelated files are transmitted.
  • Starter vs. Personal API Key: The default starter key runs on Google AI Studio's free tier. If you provide your own personal Gemini API key, all AI requests are processed directly against your personal Google AI Studio account with your dedicated quota and privacy terms.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: This application contains zero third-party advertising scripts, tracking pixels, or marketing analytics.

ℹ️ App Deployment & Information

This system runs entirely serverless within your Google Account. All recipe details and meal history are saved securely in your Google Drive inside the file Automated_Meal_Planner_DB.json.

Upon meal plan approval, the assistant will automatically generate:

  • Google Docs for individual recipes inside a folder called Meal Plan Recipes.
  • Prep & cook events scheduled on your default Google Calendar at your configured daily prep time.
  • A single consolidated, smart-merged shopping list Google Doc placed in a subfolder called Shopping Lists.
